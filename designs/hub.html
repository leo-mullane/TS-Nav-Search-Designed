Design A

Linear

White throughout, black type in light weights, hairline rules, and one turquoise line to show where you are. The search boxes are a single underline rather than a box. No colour blocks at all.

After: Linklaters, for being clean, simple and intuitive.

Design C

Dark with accent

The black header the site already has, menus that open in Oxford blue, and turquoise as the one accent. Search opens full screen. The dark stays in the header and the menus; every page below is white, so it frames the site rather than filling it.

After: Deloitte and EY, for dark colours with a strong accent and very clear layout.

What all three share

  • The approved navigation, unchanged. Services, Find a lawyer, Our thinking, Our firm and Join us, with Contact us as the only other link. The one layout difference is Design B’s tabs, which present the same three groups of links.
  • Colour. Black, Oxford blue and turquoise, with tints. No aubergine, fossil or copper, and no heavy blocks of colour on the pages themselves.
  • Type. Cera Pro, in lighter weights than the current site, with hairlines in place of heavy rules.
  • Imagery. One image, on the featured item in Our thinking, as the wireframe lays out. It would be uploaded by Travers Smith’s editors.
  • Iconography. None in the navigation.
  • Search. Every search feature from the wireframes, with filters in a sidebar.

Stand-ins

  • Font. Cera Pro is licensed, so the prototype uses Outfit, a similar free face, wherever Cera Pro is not installed. The real build would use Travers Smith’s own font files.
  • Logo. The Travers Smith name is set in type as a stand-in for the logo file.
  • Pictures and people. Tinted blocks mark where editor-uploaded images and portraits go. People, insights and events are fictional.
