Search

One indexSite search, the People directory and the Knowledge index are all this page. People and Knowledge are the same search with a scope applied before anyone types, so they keep their own entry points but share the ranking, synonyms and typeahead (R20, R28).

RankingPeople and service pages rank above insights and news; PDFs rank below the firm’s own pages. The weights are configuration, not code — see them (R21, R42).

ShareableEvery query, filter, sort, page and layout is in the address bar. Copy it, bookmark it, or put it in a campaign email and it opens this exact, live view (R27).

FiltersFilters sit in a sidebar, as Travers Smith preferred. On a phone they fold into a ‘Filter results’ panel above the results.

Filters only when they helpA filter group appears only when it can narrow the results further. A group left with a single option disappears, and options that would return nothing are never shown (R23).

Insight formatsInsights are now quick reads, long reads, podcasts and webinars. In the prototype, today’s briefings become quick reads, articles become long reads and videos become webinars. That mapping is our assumption: please confirm it.
