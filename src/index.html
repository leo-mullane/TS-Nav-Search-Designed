Travers Smith: search and navigation prototype

A lo-fi, clickable model of the navigation and search we propose for traverssmith.com, built to be reacted to in the workshop and changed afterwards. It shows structure and behaviour only. Visual design is a later phase and is not represented here.

Version 1.4 4 November 2026 Status: Travers Smith’s navigation, for review

About the contentService and sub-service names are Travers Smith’s own, from the live A–Z. Every person, article, deal, event and document is fictional, shaped like the real site so the search can be judged against realistic content. Links that go nowhere go nowhere on purpose.

How to use it in the roomThis is ClerksWell’s proposal, built from the review’s findings, and it is here to be changed. The options it does not take are still recorded in the observations report, and the room can ask for any of them.

ScopeEverything in this prototype is navigation or search: the header and its menus, the footer, the search box, the results page and the two section searches that exist today (People and Our thinking, today’s Knowledge). Page templates and the components on them are not changed. Service, profile and other pages appear only as placeholders for the menus and search to land on.

What changed in version 1.4

Travers Smith’s feedback of November 2026 asked for something cleaner and simpler, leading with the client rather than the firm’s internal structure, and set out the navigation it wants. This version builds that navigation and nothing else, so the four switchable models are gone. They are kept at the tag v1.3-models if anyone wants to look back.

#WhereChange
1Main menuServices, Find a lawyer, Our thinking, Our firm, Join us. Contact us is the only other link in the header.
2Services menuLeads with clients (asset managers, public companies, private companies, financial institutions), then services (transactions, complex advisory, disputes and investigations, full A–Z), then ‘More than law’. A search box finds any service, sub-service or sector.
3Find a lawyerA plain link to the People page, with no menu, as Macfarlanes does it.
4Our thinkingReplaces Knowledge: five trending topics, insights by format (quick reads, long reads, podcasts, webinars, events), a larger featured item and a sign-up button.

New pagesThe client, service-group and ‘More than law’ links lead to pages that do not exist yet. Each one opens a grey placeholder saying so. Writing them is content work for Travers Smith, outside the navigation and search scope.

Beyond a first phase: listed, not built

Proven on the same engine elsewhere: location-aware search, reference-format matching, sources outside the CMS. To be scoped: search analytics, saved searches and alerts, Word and PowerPoint text, editor-maintained synonyms, a semantic layer. The default answer for each is no; The workshop and the written feedback afterwards record the answer rather than assuming it.

How to review this

  • Yellow notes explain behaviour a screenshot cannot show. Red notes are questions we need answered. They are hidden when a page first opens; select ‘Show annotations’ at the bottom right to see them, and they stay on as you move between pages.

  • Menus open on hover, focus or click, and close with Escape. Everything works from the keyboard.

  • Narrow the browser, or open it on a phone, to see the same navigation at mobile width.

  • Requirements R01–R51 are listed in requirements/requirements.md; every block in the pages names the requirement it serves.
